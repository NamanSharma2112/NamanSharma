Health Disclaimer

Screen Time Tracker · Effective 11 November 2026

Helpful tools, not treatment.

Wellbeing tools, not a medical device

Screen Time Tracker includes features inspired by common ADHD and focus strategies — visual timers, body doubling, task breakdown, brain dumps, a dopamine menu, daily check-ins, transition warnings, hyperfocus check-ins and reflection. They are general self-help tools. They are not a medical device and do not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent ADHD or any other condition.

Talk to a professional

If you think you may have ADHD, or you’re struggling with focus, mood, sleep or phone use, please speak to a doctor or a qualified mental-health professional. Do not change any treatment or medication based on this app.

If you are in crisis

If you are thinking about harming yourself or you are in danger, contact your local emergency number now, or a crisis line in your country.
